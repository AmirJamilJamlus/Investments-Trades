# Glossary

Short definitions. The desk appends new terms here as they come up.

## Market basics

- **Ticker.** Short code for a listed company or fund (example: `AAPL`).
- **Quote.** The latest traded or displayed price for a ticker.
- **Bid.** The highest price buyers are currently offering to pay.
- **Ask.** The lowest price sellers are currently offering to accept.
- **Spread.** The gap between ask and bid.
- **Position.** Shares (or other units) you currently hold.
- **Balance.** Cash and total account value figures from the broker.
- **ETF.** Exchange-traded fund. A basket of holdings that trades like a stock.
- **Limit order.** An order that buys or sells only at your price or better.
- **Market order.** An order that seeks immediate execution at the current market price.

## Shariah-awareness terms (learner labels)

- **Riba.** Interest or usury. In equity investing, people often look at interest-heavy businesses and interest-linked income.
- **Halal / haram (investing context).** Permissible / not permissible under Islamic guidance you follow. This desk only notes possible issues. It does not rule.
- **Purification.** Giving away an estimated non-permissible portion of income (for example a slice of dividends) when a holding is mixed.
- **Activity screen.** Checking whether a company's main business involves flagged areas in your checklist.
- **Ratio screen.** Rough financial checks (debt, interest-linked assets, non-permissible income) used only for notes here.
