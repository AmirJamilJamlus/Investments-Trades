# Lesson 3. Market vs limit orders

A **market order** seeks fast execution at whatever price is available. A **limit order** sets the worst price you will accept.

## Plain idea

Market orders prioritize speed. Limit orders prioritize price control.

## What to notice

- This desk’s Phase 3 default prefers limit orders.
- Wide spreads can make market orders more expensive than you expect.
- A limit order may not fill.

## Practice

If you are willing to wait and care about not overpaying, which order type fits better, and why?
