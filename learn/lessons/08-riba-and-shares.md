# Lesson 8. Riba and shares (beginner)

**Riba** means interest or usury. Equity investing discussions often ask whether a company’s business or finances are heavily tied to interest.

## Plain idea

Owning a share is different from lending money at interest. Still, many guidance frameworks flag companies whose core business is conventional interest-based finance, or whose finances are heavily interest-linked.
