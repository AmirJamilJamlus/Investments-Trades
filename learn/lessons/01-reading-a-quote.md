# Lesson 1. Reading a quote

A **quote** is the market’s current price signal for a ticker. It is not a promise of what you will get if you buy later.

## Plain idea

When you ask Cursor for Apple’s price, moomoo MCP returns a live figure. That number can move seconds later.

## What to notice

- The **ticker** must be the company you mean (example `US.AAPL`).
- The **bid** and **ask** (if shown) tell you where buyers and sellers stand.
- A quote alone does not say whether the company is a good investment.

## Practice

In your own words, what is the difference between “the last traded price” and “the price I will definitely get if I buy right now”?
