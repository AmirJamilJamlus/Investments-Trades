# Lesson 2. Stock vs ETF

A **stock** is a share in one company. An **ETF** is a fund that holds a basket and trades on an exchange like a stock.

## Plain idea

Buying one stock concentrates your outcome in that company. Buying a broad ETF spreads that outcome across many holdings.

## What to notice

- ETFs still have a price, bid/ask, and daily moves.
- “Diversified” is not the same as “safe.”
- For Shariah notes, an ETF may be `unclear` until you know what it holds.
